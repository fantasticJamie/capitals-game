Privacy & Cookies

Guess The Capital

LAST UPDATED 8 September 2026

This page explains, in plain language, what Guess The Capital keeps on your device and why. There's no account, no server-side tracking, and nothing about you is collected or shared.

What's stored on your device

The game saves your progress on today's puzzle so you can close the tab and pick up where you left off. It uses your browser's localStorage — not a cookie — under a single key, and holds only:

  • Which puzzle number today's game is
  • The capitals you've guessed so far

Practice mode rounds are never saved — closing or refreshing simply starts a new random round.

What we don't do

  • No cookies of any kind
  • No accounts, logins, or sign-ups
  • No analytics, trackers, or advertising
  • No personal data is collected, stored, or shared with anyone

Everything the game needs to run — the list of capitals, the day's puzzle, your guesses — lives in the page itself or in your browser. Nothing is sent to a server.

Fonts

The page loads its typefaces (Fraunces, Inter, IBM Plex Mono) from Google Fonts. That means your browser makes a request to Google's servers to fetch them, the same as loading any image from another website — Google may see that request as it would any standard web request, but the game itself sets no cookies and adds no tracking on top of it.

Clearing your data

Use "Reset today's puzzle" at the bottom of the game to clear your saved progress at any time. You can also clear it yourself through your browser's site settings (sometimes called "site data" or "local storage") for this site.

Changes to this page

If what the game stores ever changes, this page will be updated to match, with a new date at the top.
