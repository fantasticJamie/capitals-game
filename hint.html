How the hints work

Guess The Capital gives you six tries to name the world capital of the day. Every guess in the game shows how far away you are and which direction to head, and the country search unlocks after three guesses. The clues on this page go a step further for anyone who's stuck: they start vague (which part of the world) and get more specific (how many letters, the country's flag, the first letter), so you can stop as soon as it clicks.

Everyone in the world gets the same capital each day, so the hints here match what you'll see in the game. Come back tomorrow for the next one.
